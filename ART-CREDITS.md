# Itachi emblem

The `itachiEmblem()` SVG in `card-visuals.js` adapts **Mangekyou Sharingan Itachi.svg** by **ShounenSuki**, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mangekyou_Sharingan_Itachi.svg).

The emblem artwork and its adaptation are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Changes: replaced the radial iris gradient with a solid crimson color, added a faint iris ring, and removed document-specific identifiers for inline reuse. The three-blade path is preserved. This notice applies to the adapted emblem artwork.

The surrounding procedural black-flame graphics are drawn locally by the application. There are no remote image requests while playing.
